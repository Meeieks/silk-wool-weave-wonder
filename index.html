The Art of the Persian Carpet

More Than a Carpet.
A Piece of History.

Persian carpets are more than floor coverings. They are expressions of craftsmanship, tradition and character, often carrying stories that span generations.

At Silk & Wool Weave & Wonder , we believe these pieces deserve to be treated with knowledge and care — whether you are looking to acquire a distinctive carpet, have one professionally cleaned, or restore a treasured piece.

We are based in Cape Town and are committed to giving every carpet the attention it deserves.
